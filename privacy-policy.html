TheSkoolApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

TheSkoolApp is a school communication application for administrators, teachers, and students. This policy explains how the app handles information needed to provide its service.

Information we collect

The app collects account details such as name, username, email address or phone number, role, school code, and profile information. Schools and teachers may add educational records, including attendance, homework, notes, tests, results, announcements, messages, and activity participation. Administrators may upload a school logo; users may upload permitted class-related files.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, connect them only to their school, deliver school communication, show attendance and test records, synchronize data between signed-in devices, and protect the service from unauthorized access.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored using Firebase services configured by the school or app operator. Data is scoped to the applicable Private School Code. The app does not sell personal information. We share data only with the school users authorized to access it, the service providers required to operate the app, or when legally required.

Security

The app requires authentication. School files are restricted to users whose authenticated account belongs to the same school. Test answer keys are protected from students and test grading is performed by a secured server function. No security system is absolute; the app operator must keep Firebase, administrator accounts, and devices secured.

Data retention and deletion requests

School data is retained while a school account is active unless the operator has a legal obligation to delete it. A user can request account deletion from Profile > Account deletion, or contact the app operator. Requests are reviewed by the school or app operator and handled according to applicable law and school policy.

Children

TheSkoolApp is intended for use through participating schools. Schools and guardians are responsible for providing any consent required for student use. The app operator should not use the app to collect information from children outside a school-authorized context.

Contact

For privacy questions, account-deletion requests, or support, email support.theskoolapp@gmail.com.
